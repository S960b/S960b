# HITCON CTF 2026

- **Dates:** October 23, 14:00 UTC - October 25, 14:00 UTC (48 hours)
- **Format:** Jeopardy, online
- **Organizer:** Hacks in Taiwan Association, Industrial Technology Research Institute; advisor - Administration for Digital Industries (moda)
- **Platform:** https://ctf2026.hitcon.org/
- **Flag format:** `hitcon{printable ascii+}`
- **Prize fund:** 1st $8,000 USD, 2nd $4,000 USD, 3rd $2,000 USD, Taiwan Star $1,000 USD
- **Our result:** team **SOC^2**, 1,666 points, 68th place out of 1,546 teams (21 teams with higher score on the scoreboard page shown; final rank with all participating teams)

Structure: `<category>/<challenge>/` with `README.md` (task context), `solve.md` (full solving
path), `scripts/` (exploits and tools), `assets/` (challenge files as shipped), `evidence/`
(captured outputs and raw artifacts).

Rules for this folder:

- `README.md` and `solve.md` are in English, they are what gets published. `README.ru.md` and
  `solve.ru.md` are the Russian copies for the owner.
- The flag is masked everywhere as `hitcon{...}`. The full flag string never appears in the
  repository anywhere, including scripts, assets and evidence.
- Hosts of live instances are masked as `<host>`.
- Heavy artifacts (multi-hundred-MB archives, kernel images) are not copied; a path reference
  and the generating command are given instead.

Writeups were prepared after the competition ended.
