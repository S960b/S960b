# Securinets CTF Quals 2026

- **Format:** Jeopardy, online qualification
- **Categories:** Web, Pwn, Forensics, Osint, Reverse Engineering, Hardware, Mobile, Cryptography
- **Challenges:** 29
- **Flag format:** `Securinets{...}`
- **Our result:** 10 of 29 solved, 3 688 points

Structure: `<category>/<challenge>/` with `README.md` (task context), `solve.md` (solving path),
`scripts/` (solvers and tools), `assets/` (task files).

Rules for this folder:

- `README.md` and `solve.md` are in English, they are what gets published. `README.ru.md` and
  `solve.ru.md` are the Russian copies for the owner.
- The flag is masked everywhere as `Securinets{...}`. The full string does not appear in the
  repository anywhere, including scripts and assets.
- Hosts of live instances are masked as `<host>`.
- Heavy artifacts are not copied, the path and the generation command are given instead.

Writeups were prepared after the competition ended.
